✍️ **Opening scene.** A supermarket fridge in France on 12 October, a thermostat pushed above 50 °C from far away, and nobody in the shop touched it. Why this caught your eye (the attacks on EU infrastructure lately).

<FridgeFigure part="temp" title="From 3 °C to 50 °C, and nobody touched the fridge" note="Illustration: the shape of the curve is sketched; the peak, above 50 °C, was reported (Guardian, 9 Nov 2026)." />

✍️ **What happened.** Four short beats, in your words: France on 12 Oct, 9–11 countries on 2 Nov, French blood banks right after, attribution on 9 Nov (Iran denies it).

<FridgeFigure part="map" title="Europe's fridges, attacked country by country" note="Dates and countries from press reports: Guardian, Euronews/AFP, AP, Deutsches Ärzteblatt. Tile map, not to scale." />

✍️ **The way in.** Not a classic hack of the company network: remote access to the fridge controls themselves, the kind technicians use for maintenance. Then ask the reader.

<FridgeFigure part="guess" title="Guess: how many fridge controls in Europe could anyone reach online?" note="Data: The Shadowserver Foundation, IoT device statistics, vendors Danfoss, Copeland and Carel, Europe, 11 Oct 2026." />

✍️ **The scale.** One line before the dots.

<FridgeFigure part="dots" title="Every dot is one fridge control anyone could reach" note="Data: The Shadowserver Foundation, 11 Oct vs 11 Nov 2026. Vendor fingerprints may include some non-fridge devices from the same makers." />

✍️ **Who reacted.** France, Belgium and Romania pulled the plug fast; Spain didn't move. Facts to use or drop: France 1,865 → 436; Romania 586 → 49; Belgium 337 → 48; Spain 166 → 162. Meanwhile the "old perimeter" (Fortinet VPN and firewall login pages) went from 263,653 to 255,447 (−3%).

<FridgeFigure part="race" title="Who unplugged after the attacks?" note="Data: The Shadowserver Foundation, daily, Aug–Nov 2026; countries with 150+ controls on 11 Oct. The late-September dip hits every country at once: a gap in scanning, not people unplugging." />

✍️ **The fix.** The blood banks lost no blood because monitoring still worked. Unplugging is the emergency brake, not the design.

<FridgeFigure part="sep" title="Separate seeing from controlling" note="Quote: Jeff Lander, Moderna, in Healthcare Packaging, 9 Nov 2026." />

✍️ **So what.** What you'd tell a CTO or a shop owner on Monday.
