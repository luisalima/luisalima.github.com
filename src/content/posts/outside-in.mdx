✍️ **Opening scene.** The first test run, 3 Nov: the DMARC answer comes back `p=none`, no reporting. Anyone could send mail as @looot.ai and nobody would know. One line on what that means; no DMARC lecture.

✍️ **What you did about it, in the safe order.** Facts: reporting on first (live by 16 Nov, policy still p=none), then enforcement: `p=reject` verified live on 18 Nov. Monitoring first so we didn't block our own mail.

✍️ **What outside-in is.** Facts: one command, a domain you own, one report (HTML + JSON + receipts). Five sections: first impression, company signals, developer signals, security footprint, agents and AI search. Plus "what to fix first" (at most 5) and a coverage table of what was checked. Public data and passive lookups only, company-level only: people data (employees, emails, phones) is blocked in code.
